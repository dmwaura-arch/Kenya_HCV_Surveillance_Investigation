# HCV Evidence Register — Kenya

## Purpose

This register documents publicly available evidence used to investigate trends in hepatitis C virus (HCV) screening, active infection and incidence in Kenya.

Every figure must be traceable to its original source. Unverified values must not be treated as established findings.

## Data Extraction Fields

For each source, record:

- Source title and organisation
- Direct URL or DOI
- Publication year
- Actual data collection period
- Indicator name and definition
- Reported value and unit
- Population and geographical coverage
- Whether data are observed, country-reported or modelled
- Uncertainty intervals, where available
- Important limitations
- Date accessed
- Verification status

## Interpretation Rules

1. Do not treat anti-HCV antibody positivity as proof of current infection.
2. Do not treat HCV RNA-positive case counts as incidence without appropriate supporting information.
3. Do not combine indicators with different definitions into a single trend.
4. Distinguish national estimates from findings in selected populations.
5. Document missing years and unavailable values rather than estimating them without a justified method.
6. Record source corrections and revisions when identified.

## Preliminary Research Question

Does the available public evidence support an increase in new, active HCV infections in Kenya?

## Current Status

Evidence collection is in progress. No conclusion about a recent nationwide increase in new active HCV infections has been established.

## Next Actions
