Kenya HCV Screening Trend Interpretation
1. Objective
To describe changes in reported HCV screening positivity in Kenya using publicly available national data for 2020, 2022 and 2023.
2. Findings
Screening positivity was approximately 2.64% in 2020.
Screening positivity declined to approximately 1.51% in 2022.
Screening positivity increased to approximately 1.73% in 2023.
Overall, screening positivity declined by approximately 0.91 percentage points between 2020 and 2023.
3. Interpretation
The available figures show an overall decline in HCV screening positivity between 2020 and 2023, with a modest increase between 2022 and 2023.
The number of positive screening tests increased from 1,333 in 2022 to 1,792 in 2023. However, the number of tests also increased from 88,398 to 103,465. Therefore, positive-test counts alone should not be interpreted as evidence of increasing infection incidence.
4. Limitations
The available series contains 2020, 2022 and 2023; 2021 is missing.
The figures describe screening positivity, not confirmed new active infections.
Changes in testing volume, population screened and testing practices may affect the observed results.
The available data do not establish a national trend in new active HCV infections.
5. Preliminary Conclusion
The available screening data do not demonstrate a sustained increase in HCV screening positivity. They are insufficient to confirm or rule out an increase in new active HCV infections nationally.
6. Source
Kenya National Syndemic Diseases Progress Report 2024. Screening figures sourced from the Ministry of Health Kenya Health Information System, July 2024.
