# References

## 1. Kenya National Progress Report 2024

**Use in this project:** National HCV screening data for 2020, 2022 and 2023.

**Source:** Kenya National Syndemic Diseases Control Council / national progress reporting.

**Report:** https://analytics.nsdcc.go.ke/estimates/PROGRESS-REPORT-2024.pdf

**Interpretation note:** Screening positivity describes the proportion of tests that were positive. It must not be interpreted as the incidence of new active HCV infections.

**Verification status:** Figures extracted for preliminary analysis. Confirm the original table, indicator definition and reporting coverage before final publication.

## 2. Akiyama et al. (2019)

**Use in this project:** Evidence on HCV antibody and RNA positivity among a specific study population in Kenya.

**Source:** PubMed record.

**Link:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31540840/

**Interpretation note:** Findings from a selected study population should not be treated as nationally representative estimates.

## 3. Kenyan HCV Transmission-Network Study (2024)

**Use in this project:** Molecular epidemiology evidence concerning HCV transmission networks in study populations in Kenya.

**Source:** PubMed record.

**Link:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39182826/

**Interpretation note:** Evidence of transmission networks in a study population does not by itself establish a national increase in incidence.

## 4. World Health Organization — Global Hepatitis Report 2026

**Use in this project:** International context on hepatitis trends, surveillance and progress.

**Source:** World Health Organization.

**Link:** https://www.who.int/teams/global-hiv-hepatitis-and-stis-programmes/hepatitis/reports/global-hepatitis-report-2026

**Interpretation note:** Any Kenya-specific incidence values must be extracted with their year, indicator definition and data type recorded.

## 5. World Health Organization — Hepatitis New Infections Indicator

**Use in this project:** Reference for interpreting hepatitis incidence indicators.

**Link:** https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/hepatitis---new-infections

**Interpretation note:** Check whether each value refers specifically to HCV and whether it is country-reported or modelled before using it in the analysis.

---

## Overall evidence principle

This investigation distinguishes HCV screening positivity, antibody positivity, RNA-confirmed viraemic infection and incidence. These measures answer different questions and must not be used interchangeably.

The analysis uses public sources and does not include confidential laboratory or patient-level data.
